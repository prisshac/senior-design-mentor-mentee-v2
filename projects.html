Acoustic-Based UAV Health Monitoring & Flight System Integration (CS)

Our project focuses on developing a pre-flight health monitoring system for drones to detect potential propeller and motor issues before flight. We use microphones, vibration sensors, and machine learning to analyze the drone’s condition and determine whether it’s safe to fly.

1 open mentee spot

TechniView (CS)

TechniView is an LMS system that ties together LeetCode-style technical interview questions with a learning management system. It allows users to practice coding problems, track their progress, and learn new concepts in a structured way.

3 open mentee spots

Volvo V70 Overlander (ME)

Transforming a 2002 Volvo V70 station wagon into a fully capable overlanding vehicle. The project involves the engineering design, analysis, and development of a custom lift kit, suspension components, protective armor, and other modifications, followed by the manufacturing, assembly, and installation of these components.

0 open mentee spots

PERCH: Platform for Effective Recovery of Copter Hovercraft (ME)

PERCH is an actuated landing platform that will be mounted to the back of a quadruped robot. The landing platform is required to stay stable and flat while the robot is in motion. It must be capable of securing and releasing the drone as well. The platform will serve as a place for a drone to land on the back of the quadruped robot, creating a multi-robot system that extends the use of the drone and creates an integrated system with extended capabilities.

3 open mentee spots
